Make your own
Sketchling!

Draw it. Snap it. Race it!
  1. 1
    Draw a creature BIG with a thick marker on white paper
    Fill the page! Colour it in with crayons if you like.
  2. 2
    Scan the QR code on the TV
    Type your first name and pick Little kid, Big kid or Grown-up. Little kids get a speed boost!
  3. 3
    Snap your drawing
    Tap Snap my drawing, check the cut-out, then tap Bring it to life!
  4. 4
    Meet your Sketchling!
    It jumps onto the TV with a name, a voice and a special power.
  5. 5
    Tap to run, swipe up to jump, ROAR!
    Tap fast with lots of fingers. Swipe up to hop over ink blots. Press ROAR, or tap Enable shouting and really roar!

What you draw changes how you race

  • Wings: fly over ink blots
  • Wheels or a round body: zoom on flat ground
  • Long legs: jump extra high
  • A big strong body: ROAR blots away

Tips for a perfect scan

  • Thick, dark lines
  • Close the outline, no gaps
  • One creature per page
  • Good light, no shadows on the paper
  • Hold the phone straight above the page
  • Write your name on the back, not the front
