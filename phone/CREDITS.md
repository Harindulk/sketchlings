## Art

All illustrations, icons, the mascot, the medal and the notebook-paper background are our own, drawn as inline SVG and
CSS in `src/ui.ts` and `src/style.css` (StarFun Games). The debug sample photo (`src/samples/monster.jpg`) is a
synthetic drawing generated by `packages/doodle-cutout/scripts/demo.ts`.
